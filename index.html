About Me

I am a PhD student in the Vision Learning and Reasoning Group at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My interests include object relighting, 3D dynamics learning, and embedded AI.

Before starting my doctoral studies, I received my Bachelor of Engineering from Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University. During my undergraduate studies, I was supervised by Prof. Rong Xiong and Prof. Yue Wang, where I focused on autonomous driving systems.
