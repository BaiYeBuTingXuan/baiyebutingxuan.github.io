About Me

I am a PhD student in Vision Learning and Reasoning Group at the Hong Kong Polytechnic University. My interests include Object Relighting and 3D Dynamics Learning, and Embedded AI.

Before starting my doctoral studies, I received my Bachelor of Engineering from Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University. During my undergraduate studies, I was under supervision of Prof. Rong Xiong and Prof. Yue Wang, where I focused on autonomous driving systems.
